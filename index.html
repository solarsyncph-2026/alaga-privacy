Alaga — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

The short version

Alaga is an offline, single-player game. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not ask for internet access. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any information about you.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (your pets, piso, items, collection and settings such as sound, language and reminders) is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app, unless your phone's own backup restores it (see "Backups" below).

Permissions the app asks for

Notifications (optional): only if you turn on reminders in Settings. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and never involve a server.

Vibration: to give small taps of feedback while you play.

Run at startup and keep awake: used by the notification feature so that reminders you asked for can still appear after your phone restarts.

The app does not use the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Save codes and sharing

Copying or sharing a save code only creates text on your device. If you share it, your phone's share menu sends it to the person or app you choose. The developer never receives it.

Links to other places

The About page has links to the developer's email and Facebook page. They open in your email app or browser, outside the game. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Backups

Depending on your phone's settings, Android may back up the app's saved data to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. That is done by Android and Google, not by the game, and you can control it in your phone's backup settings.

Children

The game is made to be suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
